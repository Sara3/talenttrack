TalentTrack: Product Brief

Updated 5 October 2026 · App version 11 · Warm Intro Knights

What it is

TalentTrack is a hiring service with its own lightweight applicant tracker. We hire and interview for clients, then hand off. Every candidate sits on one board, and each card says who runs that candidate next: the customer, a named TalentTrack recruiter, or an AI agent.

Live demo: sara3.github.io/talenttrack. No sign-in needed. Each visitor starts with demo data.

Japanese buyers: see the 日本語 tab above, and the ringi proposal overview.

FeatureWhat it does
BoardStages from Applied to Offer with a count per stage. Add, move by button or dropdown, remove, undo.
Bulk addPaste a list or a spreadsheet column and each name becomes its own card.
Stuck candidatesFlags anyone who has been in one stage for more than 5 days.
ScorecardsThree criteria per candidate, an average that updates on screen, and a side-by-side comparison grid.
Hand-offPer card: the customer, the TalentTrack team (with a named recruiter) or an AI agent continues the interview and scheduling.
Start datesStart date, ramp-up and agents-on-floor view.
AI toolsAI Recruiter (busiest stage, who waited too long), AI Scheduler, AI Interviewer (role questions and a scored recommendation) and a scripted AI Interview Agent.
Residence cardVerification status (Not started, Submitted, Verified, Expired), an owner, and a stuck badge after 5 days.
Driver licence (CDL) gateA driver can't reach Offer until "CDL verified" is ticked. An expired licence blocks the move, and one expiring within 30 days is flagged.
Joining dateOffer accepted date, notice period, expected joining date (DD/MM/YYYY) and a renege-risk badge for notice over 60 days.
LanguageEnglish and 日本語 language bar in the app.
MobileWorks on a phone with no sideways scrolling.

The pitch

“Your candidates are scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes, and good ones go cold while nobody notices. TalentTrack puts them on one board, shows who's stuck, and lets you hand any candidate to our recruiters or an AI agent to interview and schedule.”

Pricing (proposal)

One monthly plan for the board and AI agents, plus a per-hire fee only for candidates our team runs. These prices are a proposal and not yet final. All amounts are in US dollars.

Who runs the candidateWhat's includedPrice
The customerBoard, scorecards, stage alerts, start dates, unlimited users$99 per month
An AI agentEverything above, plus AI scheduling and structured first-round interviews$299 per month
TalentTrack teamA named recruiter screens, interviews and schedules, then hands off at offer$2,500 per hire, billed on the start date
  • A customer can mix all three on one board. The per-hire fee applies only to candidates run by the TalentTrack team.
  • No hire, no fee. If a hire we ran leaves within 60 days, we find a replacement at no charge.
  • The first 30 days are free, with no contract.

Build in-house or buy

For a company making 10 hires a year, building its own tracker costs about $43,800 in year one. TalentTrack costs about $11,100, including 3 hires run by our team.

Assumptions: one developer for 3 months at $12,000 a month to build, upkeep at 20% of build cost a year, and $600 a year of hosting. A full-time recruiter (about $65,000 salary plus 25% overhead) only costs less than our per-hire fee above about 32 hires a year. We can rerun this with your own numbers.

Customers

TalentTrack has one signed customer, Sahyadri Techworks Pvt. Ltd. Other companies are evaluating the live demo and are not customers. We have no customers in Japan yet, so Japanese companies are offered a scoped 30-day pilot.

What it does not do yet

  • AI: the AI tools are rule-based and scripted. They review your pipeline and run a structured interview with a score. They don't read resumes or use a language model.
  • Shared boards: data is saved in each visitor's own browser, so two people don't see the same board yet.
  • Out of scope: job posting, resume parsing, payroll and full HR management.
  • Recruiter names shown in the demo are placeholders.

TalentTrack 製品概要

2026年10月5日更新 · アプリ バージョン11 · Warm Intro Knights

概要

TalentTrackは、採用代行サービスと軽量な応募者管理ツール（ATS）を組み合わせたサービスです。弊社が選考・面接を代行し、内定段階で貴社に引き継ぎます。候補者ごとに、次の担当を「貴社」「TalentTrackチーム（担当リクルーター名を指定）」「AIエージェント」から選べます。

デモ： sara3.github.io/talenttrack（ログイン不要。初回はデモデータが表示されます）

稟議用のご提案概要（業務フロー、料金、役割分担）はこちら。

機能内容

一括登録名前の一覧や表計算ソフトの列を貼り付けて登録
滞留アラート同じ段階に5日を超えている候補者を表示

料金（案）

月額プランに加え、弊社チームが担当した候補者についてのみ成果報酬をいただきます。以下は提案段階の価格であり、確定したものではありません。金額は米ドル建てです。

担当内容料金
貴社ボード、評価シート、滞留アラート、入社管理、利用者数無制限月額99ドル
AIエージェント上記に加え、AIによる日程調整と構造化された一次面接月額299ドル
TalentTrackチーム担当リクルーターが選考・面接・日程調整を行い、内定時に引き継ぎ採用1名につき2,500ドル（入社日に請求）

  • 最初の30日間は無料で、契約は不要です。

導入実績

正式な契約顧客は、Sahyadri Techworks Pvt. Ltd.の1社です。その他の企業はデモを評価中であり、顧客ではありません。日本国内にはまだ導入企業がないため、日本のお客様には範囲を限定した30日間の無償試行をご提案しています。

ご留意事項

  • AI：AIツールはルールベースで動作します。履歴書の読み取りや大規模言語モデルによる判断は行いません。
  • データの共有：現在のデモではデータは各利用者のブラウザ内に保存され、チームで同じボードを共有することはまだできません。
  • 対象外：求人掲載、履歴書の解析、給与計算、人事管理全般の機能はありません。
  • 担当者名：デモに表示されるリクルーター名は仮のものです。
