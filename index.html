      Beyond candidate tracking

      TalentTrack also comes with an AI Recruiter, an AI Scheduler and an AI Interviewer, plus an autopilot that does the routine clicks for you. All of them act on the board above.

      Autopilot

      Runs for every candidate handed to an AI agent on their card. The agent interviews them as soon as they reach Interview, and these rules cover the rest. Candidates with the customer or the TalentTrack team are never moved automatically.

          How we work with you

          1. We run the pipeline

            We source and screen candidates for your open roles and keep every one of them on your board, with how long they have been waiting.

          2. We do the interviews

            Our interviewers and the AI Interview Agent run structured interviews and score Empathy, Troubleshooting and Written communication, so you compare candidates on the same scale.

          3. You choose who carries on

            Every card has a hand-off for the interview and scheduling: you keep the candidate, assign them to a named recruiter on the TalentTrack team, or give them to an AI agent. Switch at any stage, and the hire reaches your team with their scorecards and start date, wherever you work.

          How it works

          1. Add a candidate

            Type a name and a role. They land in Applied, ready to move.

          2. Move them forward

            One button sends a candidate to the next stage. The dropdown sends them to any stage, including back.

          3. See who is stuck

            Every stage shows its count. Anyone who sits in one stage longer than your limit gets flagged.

          Is TalentTrack a fit?

          It fits if you

          • Hire for a few roles at a time and track them in a spreadsheet or an inbox
          • Want to answer "where is everyone?" in ten seconds
          • Lose candidates because nobody noticed they were waiting
          • Want something a hiring manager can use without training

          Look elsewhere if you

          • Need job-board posting, resume parsing or e-signatures for offers
          • Need payroll, onboarding or a full HR system
          • Rarely hire, so there is no pipeline to track
          • Need a long list of custom features before you can start

          Need one more thing?

          Every team hires a little differently. Tell us the one thing you need on top of the basics. If it is small, we build it into TalentTrack and it keeps working for you from then on.
